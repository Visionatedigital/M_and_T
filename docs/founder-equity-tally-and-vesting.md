# Founder Equity Tally and Vesting Framework

This document describes a general tally system for dividing founder equity in a company. It separates contribution scoring from final ownership so the discussion is based on clear categories instead of informal negotiation.

This is a planning document, not legal advice. Any final equity agreement should be reviewed by a qualified lawyer before being signed.

## Final Working Allocation

The current working company ownership split is:

| Allocation | Company Share |
| --- | ---: |
| Founder A / CTO | 40% |
| Founder B / CEO | 23% |
| Founder C / COO | 7% |
| Investment Pool | 30% |

The founder pool is therefore 70% of the company, with 30% reserved for investors, future fundraising, strategic partners, employees, advisors, or other company needs.

## Core Principle

Founder equity should reflect:

- Value already created
- Capital already invested
- Risk and opportunity cost carried by each founder
- Product, technical, design, business, and operational contributions
- Expected future commitment to the company

Ideas alone should not receive the same value as execution. If multiple founders shaped the vision, strategy, product direction, roadmap, curriculum structure, or business model, those points should be shared.

## Tally Formula

The contribution tally is scored out of 100 points.

```text
Founder Company Share = Founder Tally Score / 100 x Founder Pool
```

Because the founder pool is 70%, the target tally scores for the current split are:

| Founder | Founder Pool % | Company Share |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Founder A / CTO | 57.14% | 40% |
| Founder B / CEO | 32.86% | 23% |
| Founder C / COO | 10.00% | 7% |
| Investment Pool | - | 30% |

## Contribution Categories

A reusable company tally can use categories like these:

| Category | Typical Weight |
| --- | ---: |
| Product / Technical Creation | 30-45 |
| Capital Invested | 5-20 |
| Vision & Strategy | 10-20 |
| Business Development / Sales | 10-25 |
| Operations / Administration | 5-15 |
| Design / Brand / Content | 5-15 |
| Risk / Opportunity Cost | 5-15 |
| Future Commitment | 10-25 |

The total category weights should always equal 100.

## Current Contribution Logic

The current allocation recognizes the following assumptions:

- Founder A / CTO created the core product, including technical architecture, web/mobile development, AI integrations, tooling, and design/UI work.
- Founder A / CTO also contributed to product vision, business development, and strategy.
- Founder B / CEO contributed to product direction, vision, strategy, capital, and business development.
- Founder C / COO contributed to operations, some capital, some business development, and a small amount of product/technical input.
- Founder C is intentionally capped at 7% company ownership.
- Founder B is intentionally targeted at 23% company ownership.
- Founder A remains the largest shareholder because the current company value is strongly tied to product creation and execution.

## Vesting

The agreed share should not necessarily be fully owned on day one. Vesting protects the company if a founder leaves early or stops contributing.

A common founder vesting structure is:

```text
4-year vesting
1-year cliff
Monthly vesting after the cliff
```

This means:

- A founder earns no permanent equity if they leave before the 1-year cliff.
- At the 1-year mark, 25% of their allocated shares vest.
- The remaining 75% vests monthly over the next 36 months.
- Unvested shares return to the company if the founder leaves.

## Vesting Example

Using the current allocation:

| Founder | Full Share | Vested After 1 Year | Monthly Vesting After Cliff |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Founder A / CTO | 40% | 10% | 0.833% per month |
| Founder B / CEO | 23% | 5.75% | 0.479% per month |
| Founder C / COO | 7% | 1.75% | 0.146% per month |

For example, if Founder B leaves after 18 months:

```text
5.75% vested at month 12
+ 0.479% x 6 additional months
= approximately 8.62% vested ownership
```

The unvested portion would return to the company.

## Past Work Credit

If a founder has already created meaningful value before the agreement is signed, some of their shares can be treated as immediately vested.

Example structure:

| Founder | Full Share | Suggested Immediate Vested Range | Subject to Vesting |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Founder A / CTO | 40% | 10-15% | 25-30% |
| Founder B / CEO | 23% | 3-5% | 18-20% |
| Founder C / COO | 7% | 0-1% | 6-7% |

This recognizes past work while still protecting the company going forward.

## Recommended Agreement Terms

The final founder agreement should clearly define:

- Each founder's total allocated share
- How much is immediately vested, if any
- Vesting schedule and cliff period
- What happens if a founder leaves voluntarily
- What happens if a founder is removed for cause
- Whether unvested shares return to the company or founder pool
- Whether shares are subject to company repurchase rights
- Founder roles, responsibilities, and expected time commitment
- Decision-making rights and board/control structure
- Treatment of intellectual property created before and after incorporation
